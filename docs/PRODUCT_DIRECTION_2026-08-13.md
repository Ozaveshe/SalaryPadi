# SalaryPadi product direction

Status: operating direction for the next 90 days
Reviewed: 2026-08-13

## The product in one sentence

SalaryPadi is the daily career workbench for African professionals: find a
role you can actually apply for, judge the evidence around it, and move the
application or offer one step forward.

The launch experience remains Nigeria-first. Africa expansion is a source-
rights and coverage programme, not a label placed on generic remote jobs.

## Current baseline

The production snapshot captured on 2026-08-13 showed:

- 235 public database jobs from 25 contributing source adapters;
- 33 jobs posted in the previous seven days and 93 in the previous 30 days;
- a partially available public catalog because one reviewed secondary source
  was stale;
- 35 jobs/day of evidenced authorized capacity against the 50/day operating
  target;
- effectively no proven candidate activation yet: private profiles, saved
  jobs and applications were still empty in the tiny live account cohort.

That makes the order of work clear. More listings matter, but reliable fresh
supply and a useful return loop matter before a vanity inventory total.

## Primary user and job to be done

The primary user is a time-pressed mobile job seeker in Nigeria who may also
be eligible for Africa-wide or worldwide remote work. They need to answer four
questions quickly:

1. Can I apply from where I live?
2. Is this role current and is the destination the employer's real process?
3. Is the pay and employer evidence strong enough to spend time applying?
4. What is the next action in my search today?

## Core product loop

```text
Discover -> verify fit -> save or apply -> track the next action ->
compare an offer -> contribute useful evidence -> return to better matches
```

Every signed-in surface should shorten that loop. The dashboard is not a
reporting page; it is the place that selects the next truthful action from the
user's own dates, statuses and preferences.

## Product pillars

### 1. Fresh jobs with explicit reach

- Prioritise first-party employer submissions, authorized employer ATS boards,
  licensed partner feeds and documented public APIs.
- Never scrape LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, search-result pages or protected
  sites to manufacture inventory.
- Preserve source, destination, checked date, eligibility evidence and rights
  policy on every published role.
- Measure fresh jobs, unique employers, source concentration and eligible
  country coverage—not only total rows.

### 2. A job-search workbench

- Make search fast on a small screen and keep advanced controls in document
  flow.
- Preserve a save intent through sign-in and make the shortlist useful for a
  decision, not merely storage.
- Let candidates track an application even when the role originated outside
  SalaryPadi, clearly labelled as user-entered private evidence.
- Put overdue actions, stalled applications, interviews and offers ahead of
  passive totals on the dashboard.

### 3. Pay and company decision support

- Connect a role to source-labelled company, salary, benefits and pay-
  reliability evidence without filling missing facts.
- Carry recorded job context into scam checking, salary conversion, take-home
  pay and offer comparison.
- Keep calculations versioned, explainable and jurisdiction-specific.

### 4. Africa expansion with gates

- Build measured country-rights inventories for Ghana, Kenya and South Africa
  while those packs remain non-public.
- Activate a country only after its existing job-count, source-diversity,
  explicit-eligibility, content and contribution gates pass.
- Prefer a strong country experience over a thin continent-wide directory.

## 90-day sequence

### Days 0-30: reliability and activation

- Keep every scheduled source lane healthy and remove wasted claim/fetch work.
- Eliminate false zero and false empty states in private workspace reads.
- Make the dashboard lead with one to three evidence-backed next actions.
- Preserve save intent through authentication.
- Instrument the privacy-safe activation funnel: sign in, create profile or
  save, first application or alert, and seven-day return.

### Days 31-60: supply and retention

- Expand the approved ReliefWeb metadata window and repair large-board ATS
  handling.
- Add current, corroborated employer boards only after the existing rights
  review and destination checks pass.
- Launch a written-permission employer feed onboarding path.
- Add private shortlist notes/review dates and external application tracking.

### Days 61-90: recommendations and country readiness

- Rank recommendations by explicit country eligibility first, then profile/CV
  evidence and freshness.
- Publish a country-readiness dashboard for Ghana, Kenya and South Africa.
- Activate only the first pack that passes every gate and has a credible
  ongoing supply owner.

## Metrics

North star: **verified eligible application clicks per active job seeker**.

Leading measures:

- fresh eligible jobs added per day;
- active jobs and unique employers by country;
- median posting age and apply-link failure rate;
- top-one and top-three source/employer concentration;
- search-to-detail, detail-to-apply and save rates;
- profile, alert and tracker activation;
- seven-day returning job seekers;
- applications with a recorded next action;
- offers opened in a decision tool.

## Explicit non-goals

- A social feed, generic professional network or engagement-driven timeline.
- AI-generated vacancies, employer claims, salaries, reviews or activity.
- A recommendation score that hides eligibility or missing evidence.
- Country launches justified by URLs alone rather than ongoing authorized
  supply.
- Total job count as the primary product-success metric.
