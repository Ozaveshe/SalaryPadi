# SalaryPadi growth and marketing plan

Status: operating plan for the next 90 days
Reviewed: 2026-08-13

## Positioning

SalaryPadi helps Nigerian professionals find roles they can actually apply
for, verify the evidence around the opportunity, and keep the next career
decision moving.

The promise is not "every job in Africa." The promise is a useful set of
current, source-attributed opportunities with honest eligibility, plus the pay
and application tools needed to act on them.

### Message hierarchy

1. **Find jobs open to you.** Eligibility evidence is shown before a user
   spends time applying.
2. **Know where the listing came from.** Every role keeps its source, checked
   date and application destination visible.
3. **Make the next decision.** Save, track, check, compare or estimate without
   leaving the career workflow.
4. **Built for Nigeria, expanding across Africa deliberately.** New country
   coverage follows real supply and rights review.

Avoid claims such as "Africa's largest job site," "verified employer," "live
salary," or "guaranteed safe" unless the product has evidence for the exact
claim on the exact record.

## Primary audiences

### 1. Active Nigerian job seekers

People applying now, especially on mobile, who need current Nigeria-based or
explicitly Nigeria-open remote roles. The primary call to action is to search,
save a role, or track an application.

### 2. Career improvers

People considering a move, negotiating an offer or trying to understand pay.
The entry points are salary evidence, take-home pay, currency conversion,
offer comparison and practical guides. The return path leads to relevant jobs
or a private next action.

### 3. Employers and source partners

African employers, recruiting teams and job-community operators that want an
accurate, attributable distribution channel. The primary call to action is a
reviewed employer profile, moderated vacancy submission, or written-permission
ATS/feed onboarding.

## The three growth loops

### Eligible-job loop

```text
Fresh source-attributed role -> useful job page -> apply/save/track ->
private next-action reminder -> return to current matching roles
```

Distribution should link to the most specific useful job or collection page,
not to the homepage by default. A role is promoted only while the public page
still shows current source and apply-link evidence.

### Career-evidence loop

```text
Search question -> practical guide or calculator -> explained result ->
relevant jobs or salary evidence -> contribution -> stronger future evidence
```

Every guide needs one primary action and one next decision. Calculators should
show assumptions and dates, then route users into a real career task rather
than ending at a number.

### Employer-supply loop

```text
Employer profile or vacancy -> attributable candidate traffic ->
source-quality report -> renewed permission or more roles -> broader supply
```

The employer pitch is quality and trust, not unqualified reach. Report only
measured, privacy-safe outcomes such as eligible listing views, outbound apply
clicks and stale/broken-link alerts.

## Channel plan

### Search

- Use the first ten guides as a tightly linked launch cluster around job
  eligibility, applications, salary interpretation and offer decisions.
- Publish role, city, company and country pages only when real inventory or
  evidence makes the page useful. Empty combinations stay out of the index.
- Keep canonical URLs, dates, authorship/review ownership, structured data,
  sitemap inclusion and internal links synchronized.
- Refresh pages when the underlying rule, source or inventory changes; do not
  change dates merely to imply freshness.

### Email and private alerts

- Ask for an alert after a user has formed a meaningful search, not before.
- The digest should lead with newly eligible roles and clearly separate
  Nigeria-open, Africa-open, worldwide and unclear eligibility.
- Add one private next action from the user's tracker when it exists.
- Send nothing when the source policy forbids email redistribution or the
  snapshot is stale.

### WhatsApp and community distribution

- Prepare a concise, manually reviewed daily or twice-weekly shortlist for
  partner communities. Link to SalaryPadi evidence pages rather than copying
  full third-party descriptions.
- Pilot with job-seeker communities, alumni groups, bootcamps, professional
  associations and university career offices.
- Give each partner a tagged link and a plain-language distribution policy;
  do not expose member-level behavior to the partner.

### LinkedIn, X and short-form social

Use repeatable evidence-led formats:

- "Open to Nigerians this week" with the eligibility wording and checked date;
- one practical salary or offer decision explained with visible assumptions;
- a job-scam warning-sign lesson that avoids accusing a named employer;
- a transparent coverage update: new employers, freshness and countries, not
  a vanity total;
- a useful excerpt from a guide with one specific next action.

Each post should have a single destination and should be withdrawn or updated
when its underlying job is no longer current.

### Partnerships and employer acquisition

- Build a list of Nigeria-focused employers and Africa-wide teams already
  using a supported public ATS.
- Offer a no-cost source-quality review: destination integrity, eligibility
  clarity, freshness and structured fields.
- Ask for written distribution permission covering acquisition, public
  display, storage, indexing, email and structured data separately.
- Give approved employers a clear takedown route and a monthly source-quality
  summary.
- Use successful pilots as factual case studies only after the employer and
  privacy review approve the exact numbers and wording.

### Paid acquisition

Do not scale paid traffic during the reliability phase. First establish a
measured baseline for search-to-detail, detail-to-apply, save/track activation
and seven-day return. Small keyword or retargeting pilots can begin only after
the landing route has fresh inventory, reliable attribution and a working
return loop. Set spend and success thresholds from that baseline rather than
inventing them in advance.

## 90-day execution

### Days 0-30: make the promise credible

- Stabilize scheduled supply and publish health/freshness internally.
- Launch the ten-guide search cluster with clean metadata and internal paths.
- Replace generic social posts with the evidence-led formats above.
- Add privacy-safe funnel events for search, detail, apply, save, alert,
  tracker activation and seven-day return.
- Recruit five to ten community partners for a manual distribution pilot;
  this is an outreach target, not a claim of current participation.

### Days 31-60: build repeatable return paths

- Start the reviewed job shortlist and measure which eligibility segments
  generate useful detail and apply activity.
- Add saved-search and tracker prompts to lifecycle messaging.
- Publish refreshes and follow-up guides from measured search demand rather
  than producing unrelated volume.
- Begin written-permission outreach to employers with current supported ATS
  boards and clear Nigeria/Africa hiring evidence.

### Days 61-90: expand what is proven

- Double down on the channel and content cluster that produces activated and
  returning users, not merely impressions.
- Package an employer/source-quality report and convert the best outreach
  conversations into reviewed supply pilots.
- Prepare Ghana, Kenya and South Africa launch briefs from measured inventory,
  employer diversity and rights readiness. Market only a country whose public
  product gate has passed.

## Weekly operating review

Review one funnel, one supply scorecard and one content scorecard together:

- fresh eligible jobs, unique employers, median age and source concentration;
- search-to-detail, detail-to-apply, save/track activation and seven-day
  return;
- indexed useful pages, non-brand search clicks, guide-to-product actions and
  stale-content failures;
- partner visits that become useful product actions;
- employer outreach, permission status, source readiness and takedowns;
- message or campaign variants with their exact measurement window.

Keep raw counts beside rates and label missing analytics as unavailable. Never
turn a failed analytics read into a zero.

## Launch checklist

- Product promise matches what the live jobs catalog can prove.
- The promoted route is useful at 360 px and passes keyboard/focus checks.
- Source, eligibility and checked date are visible.
- Campaign links use a documented source, medium and campaign convention.
- Consent-gated analytics fires only after the required consent.
- Every guide and campaign has one product action and a named owner.
- Email/community redistribution is allowed by the source policy.
- Stale jobs and stale claims have a withdrawal path.
- Results are reported with dates, denominators and known gaps.
